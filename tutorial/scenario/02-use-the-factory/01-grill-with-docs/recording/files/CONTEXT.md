# Blackbelt

Where consultants make their skills visible and grow them, belt by belt, in each discipline.

## Intent

- **No exam, no certificate mill.** Objectives are tailored by the mentor for each belt; Blackbelt
  never prescribes or automatically checks them. A human always validates.

## Language

### Progression

**Discipline**:
A field of expertise in which consultants progress independently (Software Craftsmanship, Cloud &
DevOps…).
_Avoid_: domain, track, skill

**Colour**:
A rank within a discipline, in the fixed order WHITE → ORANGE → GREEN → BLUE → BROWN → BLACK. A
consultant **holds** a colour once a belt of that colour is validated.
_Avoid_: grade, level, certification

**Belt**:
One consultant's attempt at one colour in one discipline, with its own mentor, objectives and
lifecycle. A refused belt is final: the consultant requests a new belt of the same colour. A
consultant has at most one **open** belt (neither validated nor refused) per discipline. It belongs
to one consultant: the **belt's consultant**.
_Avoid_: using "belt" for the rank (that's a colour); "holding" a belt (a consultant holds a colour)

**Belt lifecycle**:
Requested → mentor assigned → in progress → awaiting validation → validated / refused.
Forward-only: no withdrawal, no reopening, no step back.

**Submission**:
The belt's consultant declares their in-progress belt done, once every objective is validated, and
hands it over for a sensei's decision (→ awaiting validation). Not addressed to a particular sensei:
any sensei may decide.

**Decision**:
The sensei's final verdict on a belt awaiting validation: validated or refused.
_Avoid_: approval, review

**Objective**:
A concrete goal the mentor sets for one belt, validated by the mentor when the consultant achieves
it in real work. Added and validated only while the belt is in progress; frozen once submitted.
_Avoid_: criterion, task, exercise

### People

**Consultant**:
A person who requests belts and climbs colours.

**Mentor**:
The person assigned to coach a belt; sets and validates its objectives. Never the belt's own
consultant. Not scoped to a discipline — a deliberate simplification.
_Avoid_: coach, tutor

**Sensei**:
The person who takes the final decision on a belt (validated or refused). Never decides a belt they
mentored nor their own (rule for a later story). Not scoped to a discipline — a deliberate
simplification.
_Avoid_: validator, judge, reviewer

A person may hold several roles (e.g. consultant and mentor), but never two roles on the same belt.
