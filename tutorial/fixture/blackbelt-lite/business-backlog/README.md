# Business backlog

The project's **user stories** — the "what" and the "why" — owned by the product side. One story
per markdown file, named after its reference (`BELT-US-1-<slug>.md`).

Conventions:

- A `Status:` line near the top: `Ready` (selected, an agent may start designing it), `In design`,
  `In review`, `Done`.
- Comments (e.g. the links to the technical tickets created for the story) are appended at the
  bottom, under a `## Comments` heading.
- Layout of a story: title + INTENTION, CONTEXT, EXPECTED BEHAVIOR, BUSINESS RULES, ACCEPTANCE
  CRITERIA, OUT OF SCOPE, DEPENDENCIES.
